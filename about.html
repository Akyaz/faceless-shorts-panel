faceless-shorts

A private publishing tool for the Quick Recap Channel on YouTube.

faceless-shorts produces short news recaps and history videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel. It is operated by the channel owner only and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Prepares videos from public news headlines and openly licensed images.
  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Does not read, change or delete any other YouTube content or account data.
