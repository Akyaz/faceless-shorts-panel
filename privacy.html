Privacy Policy

faceless-shorts · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who uses this app

faceless-shorts is a private tool used only by the owner of the Quick Recap Channel to upload videos to that channel. It is not offered to other users.

Google user data we access

The app requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It is used only to upload videos, with their title, description and tags, to the owner's own YouTube channel. The app does not read, modify or delete any other YouTube or Google account data.

How data is stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in the owner's private build environment and is used only to perform uploads. No Google user data is stored anywhere else.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's issue page.
